About us

Seafood from Norwegian waters, delivered with confidence

Mearva Seafood connects buyers around the world with the cold, clean waters of the Norwegian coast. We source directly from trusted producers and manage every shipment end to end.

Our focus is simple: consistent quality, dependable volumes and full traceability from the source to your port. We work with importers, wholesalers and processors who value a partner they can rely on.
